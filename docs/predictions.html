How good is the model?

The model rates every player from their ranking points before the event and how they've played in past matches, then works out each pair's chance. To test it fairly, it was only scored on tournaments it had never seen: trained on Mumbai to predict Goa, then on Mumbai and Goa to predict Kochi.
