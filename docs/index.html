How predictable are matches?

Most IPT matches are a race to 8 games, so every game is a small contest. The model rates each player from their ranking points before the event plus what their past games show, then turns the difference between two teams into a win probability. It was tested only on tournaments it had never seen: trained on Mumbai 5.0 to predict Goa GS 11.0, then on both to predict Kochi 2.0.

How this was made

  • All data comes from indianpadeltour.in and its public API, collected one page at a time with a 1.5 second pause. Match-level results exist for three finished tournaments; ranking results go back to December 2024.
  • The points shown next to each team in a draw are players' current points, so they already include the result. Every prediction here uses points rebuilt from dated ranking results instead.
  • The expiry rule is not published. Both plausible rules are shown rather than guessing between them.
  • Most players have played a single event, so for them the model relies on ranking points alone. Walkovers and retirements are excluded.
